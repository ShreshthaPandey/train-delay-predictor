Ab tak ka summary
1. Dataset
Kaggle ka Indian Railways Delay Dataset hai, 4 files ka: delay, schedule, train details aur station names.
Delay file me 38,428,703 rows hain, 8 Feb 2025 se 7 Feb 2026 tak (poora ek saal), poore India ke trains ka.
2. Cleaning (DuckDB se, kyunki data bada tha)

Mahine-wise check me Dec/Jan me median delay 20 min mili, jabki garmi aur barsaat me 14 se 15 min thi. Matlab fog ka asar dikhta hai aur data asli lagta hai.
train_no ko 5-digit text banaya (jaise '00961'), taaki files sahi jud sakein.
3. Scope: sirf Northern Railway (NR)
Zone-wise ginti me NR sabse bada nikla (sabse zyada rows, trains aur stations).
Wo trains chuni jinke 70% ya zyada stops NR me hain. Ye 895 trains thi, jinme se 684 ka delay data mila.
Isse 3,452,134 rows bani, jo delay_nr.parquet me save hain aur pandas me aaram se chalti hain.
Baad me baaki zones ke liye wahi pipeline dobara chalayenge.
4. Schedule jodna (ek galti sudhari)
Shuru me maine station_no ko station code samjha, jo galat tha. Wo route me stop ka sequence number hai.
Fir maine station_no hata kar merge kiya, wo bhi galat nikla, kyunki kuch special trains ka route dono files me alag hai. Isse galat distance mil raha tha.
Final tarika: train_no + station_no + station_name teeno par merge. Isse sirf wahi rows judti hain jinka route sach me match karta hai. Bachi hui 12.13% rows me distance_from_origin khali hai, jo galat value se behtar hai.
